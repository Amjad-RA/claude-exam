FSD

Frontend System Design

How to design frontends that are fast, secure, reliable and accessible at scale. It covers networking and communication, security, testing, performance, storage and caching, logging and monitoring, accessibility, offline support, and low- and high-level design.

Modules: 12 Framework: Any; the ideas are framework-agnostic Interview rounds: LLD, HLD and machine coding

Sections follow the module list of the Namaste Frontend System Design course on NamasteDev. Notes are added one module at a time.
